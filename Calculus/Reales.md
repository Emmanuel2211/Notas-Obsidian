#### Points
The language that describes sets of points did not develop until late $XIX$. The ideas in this section are all very geometric, the definitions may be hard to remember without some associated picture.

![[Real Numbers 2026-04-30 14.03.59.excalidraw|center]]

The simplest types of sets are intervals. We call
$$
[a,b] =  \{  x : a\leq x \leq b \}
$$
a closed interval, and
$$
(a,b) = \{ x: a< x < b \}
$$
an open interval.
